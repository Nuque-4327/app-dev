## Tokyo Ghoul
*Tokyo Ghoul is a series about a teenager who was forced to face the heavy realities of life in a world where humans and ghouls coexist. After a fateful encounter, he had changed into a hybrid; half-human, half-ghoul, and now has to survive in a society where he cannot fully belong to either side.*
> Seasons: 4
> Protagonist: Kaneki Ken
---

The power system in this anime revolves on **kagune**. There are several types of kagune, depending on their Rc types:
- **Ukaku**: `manifests on the upper back, near the shoulder blades`
- **Rinkaku**: `manifests on the lower-middle back, near the waist`
- **Koukaku**: `manifests between the upper and lower back, below the shoulder blades. They often extend up to the wielder's arm`
- **Bikaku**: `manifests on the lower back, close to the tailbone. It has an appearance similar to a tail due to its location and length`
